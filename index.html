How the rewards system works

          
This is the in-app ruleset. If you follow it honestly, the tracker becomes a real consistency game.

        

        
      

      

        

          1. XP system
          
    
            
  • Each core task gives XP.
    • 
            
  • Each optional / HW task also gives XP.
    • 
            
  • Focus bonuses, full-day bonuses, and perfection bonuses add extra XP.
    • 
            
  • Weekly bosses also give XP when cleared.
    • 
          

        

        

          2. Coin system
          
    
            
  • Full day = more coins than partial day.
    • 
            
  • Missed day = no coins.
    • 
            
  • Longer full streaks add small extra coin bonuses.
    • 
            
  • Weekly bosses and elite weeks also pay coins.
    • 
          

        

        

          3. Reward shop
          
    
            
  • Coins are your currency.
    • 
            
  • You can redeem rewards only if you have enough available coins.
    • 
            
  • Timed rewards start a live countdown the moment you redeem them.
    • 
            
  • Spent coins go into redemption history. No borrowing coins from future days.
    • 
          

        

        

          4. Levels
          
    
            
  • Total XP decides your level.
    • 
            
  • Each new level needs more XP than the previous one.
    • 
            
  • The system has no fixed cap, so levels do not run out.
    • 
            
  • There are 30 base rank titles, then they repeat as higher prestige tiers.
    • 
            
  • The level bar shows progress to the next level.
    • 
          

        

        

          5. Level guide
          
    
            
  • Early levels climb fast, later levels take much more XP.
    • 
            
  • Your visible rank title changes as you level up.
    • 
            
  • After the 30-title ladder ends, the app keeps going with prestige-style versions of the same title set.
    • 
            
  • This means the tracker can keep leveling for the full 2-year journey without finishing.
    • 
          

        

        

          6. Season system
          
    
            
  • The tracker runs in long 90-day seasons so the game does not feel finished too early.
    • 
            
  • Each season shows its own progress, XP, full days, and boss clears.
    • 
            
  • Season labels keep changing while your main level system continues growing forever.
    • 
          

        

        

          7. Streak system
          
    
            
  • Full-day streak counts only full core days.
    • 
            
  • No-miss streak grows on full or partial days.
    • 
            
  • Core-work streak grows when you do at least 1 core task.
    • 
            
  • Missed days threaten your streaks unless a shield saves you.
    • 
          

        

        

          8. Weekly bosses
          
    
            
  • The app rotates 4 creative weekly bosses from a larger boss pool.
    • 
            
  • Examples include focus, revision, DPP, optional work, mock tests, goals, notes, and full-day pressure.
    • 
            
  • Every cleared boss pays XP and coins.
    • 
            
  • Clear all 4 active bosses in one week to trigger an Elite Week.
    • 
          

        

        

          9. Badges
          
    
            
  • Badges unlock from real milestones.
    • 
            
  • Examples: first full day, streak goals, core totals, boss clears, mock logs.
    • 
            
  • Locked badges stay visible so you know what to chase next.
    • 
          

        

        

          10. Streak shields
          
    
            
  • Shields are earned from long full-day streaks.
    • 
            
  • They are capped, so you cannot stack too many.
    • 
            
  • If a missed day happens while you still have shields, one shield is consumed to protect the no-miss streak.
    • 
          

        

        

          11. Penalty system
          
    
            
  • If 3 missed days happen inside a 10-day window, the reward shop locks for 2 days.
    • 
            
  • You can still track tasks, but you cannot redeem rewards while the lock is active.
    • 
            
  • This keeps the game honest and punishes repeated collapse, not one bad day.
    • 
          

        

        

          12. Night auto-save toggle
          
    
            
  • By default, the app auto-finalizes every day at 11:50 PM.
    • 
            
  • You can turn this OFF from the top dashboard toggle if your study often continues after midnight.
    • 
            
  • When OFF, the app will not auto-mark the day at 11:50 PM.
    • 
          

        

        

          13. Edit any past day
          
    
            
  • The Tracker tab has a calendar date picker at the top.
    • 
            
  • Select any past date (future dates are blocked) to view or correct that day's tasks, target, notes and focus.
    • 
            
  • Everything recalculates instantly: streaks, analytics, XP, coins, bosses and badges all follow your edited record.
    • 
            
  • After midnight, you can also use it to keep working on yesterday instead of starting a new day.
    • 
          

        

        

          14. Manual save button
          
    
            
  • The Tracker tab has a manual save button at the bottom.
    • 
            
  • When you press it, the currently tracked day is saved immediately using the same full / partial / missed rule.
    • 
            
  • This is your direct manual lock-in, while 11:50 PM auto-finalize stays as backup in case you forget.
    • 
          

        

        

          15. Discipline debt + snapshot
          
    
            
  • Missed core tasks from live tracking become debt items.
    • 
            
  • You can clear debt later for bonus XP, but old debt starts applying stronger pressure.
    • 
            
  • The Rewards tab also generates an accountability snapshot you can copy and share.
    • 
          

        

        

          16. Important honesty rule
          
    
            
  • Mark tasks truthfully.
    • 
            
  • Only claim shop rewards after real work.
    • 
            
  • If you cheat the app, the game system dies.
    • 
            
  • If you use it honestly, consistency becomes addictive.
    • 
          

        

      

      

        Quick reading
        Full days are your strongest engine. Partial days keep the streak alive. Missed days trigger penalties. XP grows your identity, coins buy your rewards, bosses force weekly discipline, badges preserve long-term pride, and shields stop one bad day from destroying momentum.
